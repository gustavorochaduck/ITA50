🟢 Jankenpon

The game principle is based on a classic decision-making scenario: rock, paper, scissors. Three simple options that are mutually exclusive — a small, self-contained system with strengths and weaknesses.


Player versus computer, round by round. The ASCII graphics transform each decision not only into text, but into a kind of mini-performance. However, the core gameplay remains unchanged: the player tries to outsmart the computer’s random choice.
